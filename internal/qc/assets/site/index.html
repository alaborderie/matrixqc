What the checker catches

C0
Files and matrix rows that do not join uniquely.
C1
Expected text missing from the required consecutive frames.
C2
A foreign phone or URL — with the sibling row named when known.
C3
FPO, TBD and other placeholder or forbidden text.
C4
Unreadable, low-confidence or near-match text for human review.

What the sample proves

The deterministic harness can generate labelled errors, decode every frame, run offline OCR and attach the evidence read.

It does not validate real-file accuracy, preparation savings, willingness to pay or a turnaround promise. Hard typography can still require substantial review.
